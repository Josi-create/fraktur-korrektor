# Highlights from purchased books

Purchased Kindle books are copy-protected. Since summer 2026 the Kindle stores only the place in the book for a new
highlight, and Fraktur-Korrektor cannot read the encrypted text (see [Highlights from the Kindle](kindle.md)). Amazon
itself shows these highlights with their text, though: in the *notebook* on its reading site in the browser. From
there you take them over by hand.

## How it works

1. Open the notebook in the browser:
   - with an amazon.com account: `https://read.amazon.com/notebook`
   - with an amazon.de account: `https://lesen.amazon.de/notebook`
2. Sign in with the Amazon account the Kindle is registered to.
3. Choose the **book**. The notebook shows its highlights with the location – and the page, where the e-book has
   page numbers – together with your notes.
4. Select the text of a highlight with the mouse, copy it and paste it into a note in Obsidian. Write down the
   location or page with it, so you can find the place in the book again.

## Good to know

- **Sync first.** The notebook only knows a highlight once the Kindle has sent it to Amazon. For that it must have
  been connected to Wi-Fi after you highlighted.
- **Purchased books only.** Books you put on the Kindle yourself (for instance with *Send to Kindle*) do not appear
  in the notebook. You do not need it for them: Fraktur-Korrektor takes over their highlights itself, with the text.
- **The clipping limit.** If the publisher limits how much can be copied out of a book, the notebook does not show
  every highlight in full either.
- The addresses are as of October 2026. Amazon may change them; the start page `read.amazon.com` (or
  `lesen.amazon.de`) then leads on.
