## Sample book: Goethe, *Faust*

Eight pages for trying out the program and as test data, just as the program reads them in: page images, recognised
text (Tesseract with the Fraktur model frak2021, uncorrected), line positions and quality traffic light. Pages as in
the table above: title page (with library stamp – noise to delete), the opening of »Night« (printed pages 17–20) and
the prose scene »Dreary Day. A Field.« with hyphenation (printed pages 174–176).

**Source:** Goethe, *Faust. Eine Tragödie*, Stuttgart: Cotta 1862, scanned by the Robarts Library, University of
Toronto, on archive.org: <https://archive.org/details/fausteinetragd00goetuoft>. **Rights:** the work and this edition
are in the public domain; the scan is a mere reproduction and creates no new rights. As of 25 September 2026.
