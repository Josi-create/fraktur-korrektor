# Taking part in the beta test

*Deutsche Fassung: [BETATEST.md](BETATEST.md)*

Thank you for trying Fraktur-Korrektor before it is released as version 1.0. The program is meant for people who
work with old books and should not have to care about computers. Whether it succeeds only you can tell. Every place
where you hesitate is a finding for us, not a failure on your part.

## What you need

- A computer running Windows 10 or 11, macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, or Linux.
- About two weeks during which you spend **two to three hours** in total with the program, not necessarily in one
  sitting.
- Ideally a **book of your own** in Fraktur that you are working on anyway: as a PDF, as scans or photos, or as an
  export from Transkribus. Without one, use the example book that comes with the program.

## What to try

Please try to do this **without outside help**, using only the program and its help (F1 key). If you get stuck,
note where, and only then ask.

1. **Install.** Download and install the program: [How to install it](docs/en/install.md). Note whether Windows or
   macOS showed a warning and whether you knew what to do.
2. **First steps.** Open the example book in the library and follow [First steps](docs/en/first-steps.md).
3. **Open a book of your own.** Show the program your book with “Open …”. Is the recommendation clear? How long did
   reading it in take, and what did the traffic light say afterwards?
4. **Read and correct.** Work through a few pages the way you normally would. If you take notes, create a few
   note cards.
5. **Save.** Save the book as a PDF ([Saving a book as PDF](docs/en/pdf-sichern.md)) and open it in your usual PDF
   viewer.

## What we would like to know

- Where did you get stuck or hesitate?
- Which words, messages or buttons were unclear?
- Was there an error message? If so, its exact wording, ideally as a screenshot.
- What was missing for your work?
- Would you keep using the program, and if not, why not?

Short notes are enough. Half sentences help us more than a polished report that never gets written.

## How to tell us

- **Using the form** [Beta test feedback](https://github.com/Josi-create/fraktur-korrektor/issues/new?template=betatest.en.yml).
  You need a free GitHub account for this. What you write there is **public**.
- **Or by e-mail** to the person who invited you, if you would rather not create an account or not write in public.

Please do **not post scans from books still under copyright** publicly. A screenshot of an error message or a small
excerpt is enough.

## Your data

The program sends nothing to the internet. Your books, corrections and notes stay on your computer. We only see
what you write to us yourself.

## Afterwards

Your feedback shapes version 1.0. If you like, we will credit you as a tester in the [CHANGELOG](CHANGELOG.md).
Just tell us under which name.
